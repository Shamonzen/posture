Medium (1)

fail AD-ACC-002 Privileged accounts' passwords expire posture-dc.posture.test

An admin password that never expires is often years old, shared, or written down. Any one leak of it stays valid indefinitely, and with it full control of the domain.

ATT&CK
T1078.002

Evidence

vagrant

Remediation

Clear "Password never expires" on each listed admin account and rotate its password, or move service-style admin accounts to gMSAs.

Set-ADUser <name> -PasswordNeverExpires $false
  • https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-server/identity/ad-ds/plan/security-best-practices/implementing-least-privilege-administrative-models
